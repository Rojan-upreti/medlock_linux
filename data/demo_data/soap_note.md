# SOAP clinic note (synthetic template)

Use this for a progress note, clinic note, H&P-lite, or "write a note".

Patient: [Patient name]  DOB: [DOB]  Date: [Date]  Clinician: [Name, credentials]

S — Subjective
Chief complaint: [in the patient's words].
HPI: [onset, location, duration, character, aggravating/relieving, radiation, timing, severity].
ROS: [pertinent positives and negatives only].
Allergies: [and reaction]. Home meds: [or see list].

O — Objective
Vitals: [BP, HR, RR, Temp, SpO2, weight].
Exam: [focused systems].
Data: [labs, imaging, other results with dates].

A — Assessment
1. [Working diagnosis or problem] — [brief clinical reasoning].
2. [Additional problem].

P — Plan
1. [Further testing].
2. [Treatment the clinician will consider; do not invent doses unless the user supplied them].
3. [Follow-up interval and return precautions].
4. [Patient education provided].

Draft only — review and sign in the legal medical record.
