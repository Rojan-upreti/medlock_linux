# Patient education (synthetic template)

Use when the user wants something explained to a patient, a handout, or plain language.

Title: [Condition or treatment]
Audience: patient and caregiver. Reading level: clear short sentences.

What this is
[One paragraph.]

What we are doing next
[Tests, referrals, lifestyle, medications in general terms. Do not invent a dose or a diagnosis the clinician did not supply.]

What you can do at home
[Sleep, fluids, activity, warning signs.]

When to call or go in
Call now for [chest pain, trouble breathing, fainting, stroke signs, rapidly worsening pain, allergic reaction].

Questions for your visit
[2–3 items the patient can ask the clinician.]

This is education, not personal medical advice. A licensed clinician must tailor it.
